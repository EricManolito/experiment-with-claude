## Mortal Kombat history
Mortal Kombat is a popular fighting game franchise created by Ed Boon and John Tobias. The first game was released in 1992 by Midway Games. The series is known for its intense combat, unique characters, and graphic violence, including the iconic "Fatalities" finishing moves. Films and other media adaptations have expanded the franchise's reach and popularity. 

## Key Features of Mortal Kombat:
1. **Characters**: The series features a diverse roster of characters, each with their own unique fighting styles, special moves, and backstories. Some of the most iconic characters include Scorpion, Sub-Zero, Raiden, Liu Kang, and Johnny Cage.
2. **Gameplay**: Mortal Kombat is a 2D fighting game where players select a character and engage in one-on-one battles. The gameplay includes a variety of moves, combos, and special attacks.
3. **Fatalities**: One of the most distinctive features of Mortal Kombat is the Fatality system, which allows players to perform gruesome finishing moves on their defeated opponents.
4. **Story Mode**: Many Mortal Kombat games feature a story mode that delves into the lore of the Mortal Kombat universe, exploring the conflicts between different realms and characters.
5. **Multiplayer**: The series has a strong multiplayer component, allowing players to compete against each other locally or online.
6. **Evolution**: Over the years, Mortal Kombat has evolved with advancements in graphics, gameplay mechanics, and storytelling. The series has seen numerous sequels, spin-offs, and adaptations in other media, including movies and TV shows.
7. **Cultural Impact**: Mortal Kombat has had a significant impact on popular culture, influencing other video games and media. It has also sparked discussions about violence in video games and led to the creation of the Entertainment Software Rating Board (ESRB) to rate game content.
8. **Competitive Scene**: Mortal Kombat has a dedicated competitive gaming community, with tournaments and events held worldwide, showcasing the skills of top players.
9. **Soundtrack**: The series is known for its memorable soundtracks, which often feature intense and atmospheric music that complements the game's action.
10. **Graphics and Visuals**: Mortal Kombat has consistently pushed the boundaries of graphics and visual effects, with each installment showcasing improved character models, environments, and special effects.

Overall, Mortal Kombat remains a beloved and influential franchise in the gaming industry, known for its unique blend of action, storytelling, and memorable characters.
